# 10. FINANCIAL CALCULATION ENGINE

This section is critical.

The screenshot provides enough information to derive the main reconciliation calculation.

Use signed balances.

For a normal debit-balance bank account:

## A. Closing Balance as per Books

Use the real bank-ledger opening balance plus all posted bank-side transactions through To Date.

Conceptually:

```text
Book Closing Balance
=
Opening Book Balance
+ Total Bank Receipts
- Total Bank Payments
```

Apply account Dr/Cr sign conventions correctly.

## B. Uncleared Payments

Payments recorded in BMS but not cleared by the bank as of To Date.

```text
Uncleared Payments
=
Sum of eligible payment entries
whose Clearing Date is blank
or later than To Date
```

Display this as a positive addition in the reconciliation summary.

## C. Uncleared Receipts

Receipts recorded in BMS but not credited by the bank as of To Date.

```text
Uncleared Receipts (Signed)
=
- Sum of eligible receipt entries
whose Clearing Date is blank
or later than To Date
```

Display this as a negative amount.

## D. Closing Balance as per Bank

The screenshot supports this relationship:

```text
Closing Balance as per Bank
=
Closing Balance as per Books
+ Uncleared Payments
+ Uncleared Receipts (Signed)
```

## E. Difference

```text
Difference
=
Entered Bank Statement Closing Balance
- Calculated Closing Balance as per Bank
```

If the difference is zero:

Display:

`Difference: 0.00`

If it is non-zero:

Display the actual signed difference clearly.

Never force a difference to zero.

Do not automatically create adjustment vouchers.

If a bank charge, direct credit or missing transaction causes a difference, explain that the accounting transaction must be entered through the appropriate existing voucher workflow.
